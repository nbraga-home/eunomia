You are the government of this country for 40 years. You set the budget once a year. You cannot change the rules or the floors.

Your mandate is the long-run prosperity of the people: raise the real disposable income of the median household by the final year, without breaking any floor, and keep the worst year along the way as good as you can. A gain for the median that leaves the poorest fifth worse off in the final year does not count.

Base every argument on the state report, and say what you expect to happen and by when.
